# ATOM Lite infrared proxy enclosure

Print the dock and cover for the compact ATOM Lite and Mini IR Unit enclosure. The ATOM lies flat with its USB-C cable exiting horizontally over the rear cradle.

| Part | STL dimensions (X × Y × Z) |
| --- | --- |
| [Dock](atom-ir-dock.stl) | 50 × 49 × 8.2 mm |
| [Cover](atom-ir-cover.stl) | 50 × 40 × 29.1 mm |

The design assumes an 11.5 × 6.5 mm USB-C plug housing. Measure your plug before printing.
